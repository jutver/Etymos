# Product

## Register

product

## Users
University students, master's/PhD researchers (primary); lecturers, researchers, freelancers, SEO/content writers, agencies (secondary). Vietnamese-first, multi-language (EN/FR/JA support).

## Product Purpose
Affordable, Vietnamese-optimized plagiarism and AI-generated-content detection with explainable AI (plain-language reasons, not just a percentage) and one-click academic rewriting. Positioned as the accessible alternative to Turnitin/Copyleaks/Grammarly/Quetext for individuals Turnitin doesn't serve directly.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, energetic, precise. Credible enough for academic work, confident enough to feel like a real modern product, never sterile or clinical.

## Anti-references
Default AI-purple/indigo gradients on white. Centered-everything hero + 3-identical-feature-cards + stock CTA rhythm. Filler emoji-as-icons or clip-art. The generic "AI-generated SaaS template" look.

## Design Principles
1. Functional color is sacred — severity/result colors (red/amber/yellow/violet) never blend into the brand-blue chrome.
2. Every gate is an invitation, not a wall — locked/premium states always show what's behind them and how to unlock it.
3. Vietnamese-first, not Vietnamese-translated — Vietnamese content, diacritics, and names are the default, not an afterthought.
4. Show real product substance over decoration — real report UI previews, not fake screenshots or gradient blobs.
5. Compose with restraint, vary with intent — one palette and one type family throughout; layout variety comes from composition, not new colors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast minimum (explicit care over the blue gradient and functional-color badges), visible focus states, keyboard navigation, semantic HTML, full Vietnamese diacritic rendering across UI and content.
